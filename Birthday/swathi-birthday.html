🧭 Swathi's Adult Survival Kit
Congratulations! You are now legally an adult. Which means the government is officially excited to hand you a stack of documents, forms, and bureaucratic confusion. 🥳

Don't worry — your bestie has prepared The Ultimate Survival Guide so you don't end up Googling "what is a PAN card" at 11 PM. (Click each card to unlock the steps!)
🪪
Priority #1 · Tax
Get Your PAN Card
The government's way of saying "Welcome to adulting — we want to track every rupee you ever make." Without this little card, your bank will look at you like you asked to pay in mangoes. 🥭 Also it's THE most accepted photo ID after Aadhaar. Get it ASAP.
  • 1
    Go to protean-tinpan.com or onlineservices.nsdl.com
  • 2
    Apply for Form 49A (Indian citizens)
  • 3
    Fill in your Aadhaar, DOB, name exactly as on Aadhaar
  • 4
    Upload photo, signature & pay ₹107 fee
  • 5
    e-PAN arrives in email in ~3–5 days 🎉 Physical card in 2–3 weeks
🏦
Priority #2 · Finance
Open Your Own Bank Account
Until now, you've been asking your parents for money like "Amma, just ₹200 please 🥺". Now it's your turn to have an account balance that gives YOU anxiety. Welcome to independence! 💸 Zero balance accounts exist — use them.
  • 1
    Choose a bank — SBI, Kotak, HDFC, etc. (Kotak 811 & IDFC First are great digital options)
  • 2
    Bring: Aadhaar, PAN card, passport photo, mobile number
  • 3
    Fill KYC form at branch OR open online via their app
  • 4
    You get a debit card + internet banking login
  • 5
    Immediately set up UPI (PhonePe/GPay) — you'll need it to split bills with friends 😂
📘
Priority #3 · Travel
Apply for a Passport
Even if you have no plans to travel, a passport is basically adulting's "final form". It says "I could leave this country if I wanted to." Very powerful energy. ✈️ Also required if you ever want to attend international conferences or just flex.
  • 1
    Register at passportindia.gov.in
  • 2
    Apply for Fresh Passport (Type P, 36 or 60 pages)
  • 3
    Book appointment at nearest Passport Seva Kendra
  • 4
    Carry originals + self-attested copies of Aadhaar, DOB proof, address proof
  • 5
    Pay fee (~₹1500) & get police verification done
  • 6
    Passport arrives in ~3–4 weeks by Speed Post 🎉
🗳️
Priority #4 · Civic
Get Voter ID (EPIC)
YOU ARE NOW ELIGIBLE TO DECIDE THE FATE OF THIS NATION. No pressure! 😅 In all seriousness, voter ID is also an important photo ID and getting it is your civic duty. Plus, imagine the clout — "sorry, I have to go vote." Very adult energy.
  • 1
    Go to voters.eci.gov.in or the Voter Helpline App
  • 2
    Fill Form 6 for new voter registration
  • 3
    Upload: passport photo, Aadhaar, proof of age & address
  • 4
    Submit and wait for BLO verification (~30 days)
  • 5
    Download e-EPIC or receive physical Voter ID card
🔵
Priority #5 · Identity
Update Your Aadhaar
Your childhood Aadhaar photo looks like a passport photo from a nightmare, doesn't it? 📸 Now that you're 18, update your biometrics — fingerprints, iris, and yes, finally a photo where you actually look like yourself. The government will be delighted.
  • 1
    Visit nearest Aadhaar Enrolment Centre (usually banks, post offices)
  • 2
    Request mandatory biometric update (free for 18-year-olds!)
  • 3
    Give fingerprints, iris scan, updated photo
  • 4
    Updated Aadhaar arrives in ~90 days
  • 5
    Also link Aadhaar to your bank account for DBT benefits 💰
🚗
Priority #6 · Mobility
Driving Licence (DL)
The official permission slip to terrify everyone on the road! 🛣️ First, get a Learner's Licence — it comes with the exciting privilege of having a big "L" on your vehicle. No judgment, we've all been there. Permanent DL unlocks at 18 for a 4-wheeler!
  • 1
    Apply online at sarathi.parivahan.gov.in
  • 2
    Book slot for Learner's Licence test at RTO
  • 3
    Pass the 15-question traffic rules test (study the signs!)
  • 4
    After 30 days of learning, apply for Permanent DL
  • 5
    Pass the driving test at RTO (don't panic at roundabouts 😂)
📱
Priority #8 · Digital
Your Own SIM Card
Finally! A SIM in YOUR name means YOUR data, YOUR bills, and YOUR parents can no longer check your call records. 😌 Freedom tastes like ₹199/month unlimited data. Get a SIM in your name — it's also proof of address for many documents! Two birds, one tiny card.
  • 1
    Walk into any Jio / Airtel / BSNL store
  • 2
    Carry: Aadhaar + one passport photo
  • 3
    Fill a simple form & give biometrics (fingerprint) on-site
  • 4
    SIM activates in ~4 hours usually
  • 5
    Set up DigiLocker to store all your documents digitally 📂
